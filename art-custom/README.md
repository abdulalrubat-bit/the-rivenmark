# art-custom/ — authored art

Anything here **replaces** the forged frame of the same name in `../art/`.

Mirror `art/`'s folders and frame names:

    art-custom/bestiary/thrall-rest.png
    art-custom/bestiary/thrall-run-0.png   ... -run-7.png
    art-custom/heroes/isaac-rest.png
    art-custom/props/barrel-0.png
    art-custom/walls/top-0.png

Then `node phaser/tools/pack-atlas.js` (or anything that builds — the deploy
and every smoke suite do it for you). The packer prints how many frames are
authored and how many are still forged.

**Frame by frame is the point.** Replacing all 244 before anything renders is
not a project anyone finishes. One authored thrall gives you a delve with one
authored thrall in it and 243 forged ones, and it still runs.

**Any resolution.** `art/` is drawn at 2× and rendered at 1/2; a replacement
can be any size. The packer records its ratio to the frame it replaces and the
game divides by it, so a 4× thrall occupies exactly the world space the 2× one
did. Nothing needs to know which frames are authored.

**Keep the aspect ratio.** The packer warns about a replacement that changes
shape rather than just resolution: the game draws bodies centred on their
world position and sorts them by it, so a different shape does not sit where
the old one sat. Draw with the FEET at the centre of the canvas, not the
middle of the torso.

Straight alpha, transparent PNG. Premultiplication is the renderer's job.

The full list of what is needed — every frame, its size, and for the bestiary
its radius, role and colour — is `../art/SPEC.md`, generated by
`node tools/art-spec.js`.

Unlike `art/`, this directory is **hand-made and committed**. It is the one
art folder that is not generated.
