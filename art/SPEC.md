# The Rivenmark — art specification

GENERATED by `tools/art-spec.js`. Do not hand-edit: it is measured from
the files the game loads, so it cannot drift from them.

## The contract

- **Supersample 2.** Everything in `art/` is drawn at 2× and rendered at 1/2. A 88×88 file is 44×44 world units.
- **Authored art may be any resolution.** Drop a replacement into
  `art-custom/<folder>/<same-name>.png` at any size; the packer records
  its ratio to the frame it replaces and the game divides by it. A 4×
  thrall occupies exactly the world space the 2× one did.
- **Keep the aspect ratio.** The packer warns about any replacement that
  changes shape: the game positions by centre and sorts by foot, so a
  different shape does not sit where the old one sat.
- **Transparent PNG, straight alpha.** Premultiplication is the renderer's job.
- **One frame at a time is fine.** Nothing needs replacing all at once.

## Anchoring

Bodies and heroes are drawn centred on their world position, and sorted
against each other by that position — so the FEET should sit at the
centre of the canvas, not the middle of the torso, or a body will stand
in front of something it is behind. Standing scenery (pillars, barrels,
tombs) follows the same rule: the base at the centre, the shaft running up.

## Animation

`gait_poses` is 8. Cycles advance by DISTANCE
TRAVELLED, not on a clock: a body slowed to half speed takes half-length
steps rather than moonwalking. Draw the run as a loop that reads at any
playback rate, because there is no fixed one.

Contact sheets of every existing cycle are in `art/cycles/` — one strip
per kind, for looking at rather than loading.
